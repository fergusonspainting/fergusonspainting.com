Commercial questions

Can you work after hours or on weekends?

Yes. For stores and offices we usually work after closing or on your slow days, and we seal off the work area so you can stay open.

Do you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. Ferguson's Painting & Cleaning, Inc. is fully insured, and we'll send the certificate to you or your property manager before work starts.

Do you work with HOA and POA boards and community managers?

Yes. We paint clubhouses, common areas and amenities to the community's approved colors and schedule around residents and events. See the HOA and POA section above.

Can you give a written proposal for a board meeting?

Yes. Every estimate is written and itemized, so a board or manager can review it before approving the work.
