Painting Maitland homes

Maitland sits between Winter Park and Altamonte Springs in ZIP 32751, around Lake Maitland and a string of smaller lakes. Its neighborhoods range from mid-century ranch homes to large lakefront houses, with plenty of mature oaks overhead.

Shade and lake air keep homes cool, and they also feed mildew and wood rot. We wash, repair and repaint exteriors, update interiors, and take out the popcorn ceilings common in older homes.
