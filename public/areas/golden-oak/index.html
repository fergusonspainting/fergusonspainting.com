Painting Golden Oak homes

Golden Oak is a gated, custom-home community in Lake Buena Vista, surrounded by the Walt Disney World resort area. Homes here are built to detailed design standards, so exterior work like paint colors usually needs the community's approval before it starts.

Custom homes have more trim, columns, stonework and wood detail than typical homes. That detail looks beautiful, and it's also where sun and storms do damage first. We repair what's needed, protect every surface around the work, and leave the site clean each day.

Access and approvals

Golden Oak is gated. Put your access instructions in the quote form, and we'll work with you or your home manager to arrange entry. Get any exterior color or design changes approved by the community first. We'll paint to the approved specs.

Golden Oak questions

Do you work in Golden Oak?

Yes. Golden Oak and the surrounding Lake Buena Vista area are in our service area, along with nearby Celebration, Windermere, Dr. Phillips and Horizon West.

Do exterior paint colors need approval in Golden Oak?

Usually, yes. Golden Oak has community design standards. Get approval before work starts, and we'll match the approved colors.

Can you work while I'm away?

Yes. Many owners have second homes here. We can coordinate access and send photos when the work is done.
