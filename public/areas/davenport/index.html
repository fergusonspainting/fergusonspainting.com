Painting Davenport homes

Davenport, in northeast Polk County around the US-27 and I-4 corridor and the Four Corners area, has grown fast. Its newer stucco neighborhoods and vacation home resorts are the reason. ZIP codes 33837, 33896 and 33897 cover much of the area.

Newer homes still need care. Builder-grade paint fades and chalks within a few Florida summers, stucco develops hairline cracks as homes settle, and rental homes take heavy wear inside. We repaint, repair and wash, and we finish every job neat and clean.

Repainting a newer home?

A first repaint on a newer home is the time to upgrade from builder-grade paint. Derwin's go-to is Sherwin-Williams, and we'll use another brand if you prefer. Read the best paint for Florida stucco.

Changing colors? Most Davenport communities have an HOA or POA with an approved palette. Get approval first.

Davenport questions

Do you travel to Davenport?

Yes. Davenport, ChampionsGate and the Four Corners area are part of our service area, along with Reunion and Kissimmee.

My newer home's paint is already fading. Is that normal?

It's common. Many new homes get builder-grade paint that doesn't last long in the Florida sun. A repaint with a quality exterior paint, after proper prep, holds up much better.
