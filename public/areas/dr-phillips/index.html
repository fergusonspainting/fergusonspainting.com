Painting Dr. Phillips homes

Dr. Phillips sits in southwest Orlando around Sand Lake Road's Restaurant Row, between Windermere and the theme parks, in ZIP codes 32819 and 32836. It includes established lakeside neighborhoods and the Bay Hill golf community, with mature landscaping, big lots and many HOA or POA communities.

Homes here are often 20 to 40 years old, the age where original trim starts to rot, stucco shows settling cracks and earlier paint jobs have chalked and faded. We repair what's needed, then repaint inside or out.

Dr. Phillips questions

Do you paint in Bay Hill?

Yes. Bay Hill and the surrounding Dr. Phillips neighborhoods are part of our service area.

My older home has popcorn ceilings. Can you remove them?

Yes. Have the ceiling tested for asbestos first if the home is older. See popcorn ceiling removal.

Do I need HOA or POA approval to repaint?

Many Dr. Phillips communities require approval for exterior colors. Get approval first, and we'll paint to it.
