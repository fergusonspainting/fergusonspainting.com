Painting Longwood homes

Longwood sits between Altamonte Springs and Lake Mary in Seminole County, in ZIP codes 32750 and 32779. It has a historic district with older wood-frame homes, plus wooded neighborhoods and newer HOA or POA communities.

Older wood homes need rot repair and careful prep, and shaded stucco homes fight mildew. We repair first, wash, then paint inside or out.
