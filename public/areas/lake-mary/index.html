House painters in Lake Mary, FL

Interior and exterior painting and home repair for Lake Mary and nearby Seminole County neighborhoods.

Painting Lake Mary homes

Lake Mary's neighborhoods are mostly established, deed-restricted communities with HOAs or POAs, mature trees and well-kept streets. Shade from oaks keeps homes cool, and it also lets mildew build up on stucco and trim. Older homes start to show soft wood at fascia, door frames and garage trim.

We repaint the whole house or just the trim, fix rot and drywall damage first, and leave the job site clean every day, whether you live in the house or are getting it ready to sell.

We serve Lake Mary (ZIP 32746) and nearby neighborhoods, including Heathrow and Timacuan.

Getting ready to sell?

Fresh paint is one of the most affordable updates before listing. A neutral interior repaint, a fresh front door and trim, and a pressure-washed driveway help a home photograph well. We can also take care of the small repairs that turn up in inspections, like soft trim, drywall dings and sticking doors.
