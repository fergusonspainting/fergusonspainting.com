Careful, clean painting and repair for Windermere's lakefront and gated-community homes.

Painting Windermere homes

Windermere, ZIP 34786, wraps around the Butler Chain of Lakes in southwest Orange County. Estate home on the water? See Lake Butler. Homes range from older lakefront houses to large estates in gated communities, and most sit under HOA or POA architectural rules.

Lakefront and heavily landscaped homes face extra moisture, shade and sprinkler spray. That means mildew on stucco, rot at trim and door frames, and paint that fades fastest on the sunny sides. We fix the damage first, then repaint, and we protect landscaping, pools and docks along the way.

Gated communities and HOA/POA rules

Many Windermere neighborhoods are gated and have strict exterior standards. Get color and exterior changes approved by your HOA or POA first, and add your gate code or access notes to the quote form so we can arrange entry.

Windermere questions

Do you work in gated Windermere communities?

Yes. Add your gate code or access instructions to the quote form, and we'll coordinate entry with you or your community's gate.

Do I need HOA or POA approval to repaint?

Most Windermere communities require approval for exterior colors. Get approval first, and we'll paint to the approved colors.

Can you protect my landscaping and pool while painting?

Yes. Plants, walkways, pools and windows are covered before work starts, and we clean up every day.
