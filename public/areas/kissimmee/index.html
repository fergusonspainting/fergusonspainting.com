Painting Kissimmee homes

Most Kissimmee homes are concrete block and stucco, and many sit in HOA or POA communities with color rules and maintenance standards. Stucco holds up well, but in Osceola County's sun and humidity the paint chalks, fades and grows mildew on the shady sides of the house.

A lasting stucco repaint starts with a thorough wash, then repairing hairline cracks and priming bare areas before the finish coats go on. That prep is what keeps a Kissimmee repaint looking good season after season.

We serve Kissimmee and nearby communities across ZIP codes 34741, 34743, 34744, 34746, 34747 and 34758, including Buenaventura Lakes, Remington, downtown Kissimmee and the resort communities west toward Reunion, Reunion West and ChampionsGate.

Kissimmee painting questions

Do you paint stucco homes in Kissimmee?

Yes. Stucco is most of what we see in Kissimmee. We wash, patch hairline cracks, prime and paint.

Can you work around my vacation rental bookings?

We'll do our best. Send us your open dates and we'll plan the work around them.

My HOA or POA sent a violation letter. Can you help?

Yes. Send us the letter. Mildew and stains can often be washed off. Faded or peeling paint needs a repaint in your community's approved colors.
