We've painted in Celebration

Celebration's homes are built to a pattern book of traditional styles: Victorian, Colonial, Classical, Coastal and more. That means deep front porches, columns, railings, gable trim and lots of wood detail. Those details are what make the houses beautiful, and they're also where Florida weather does the most damage.

Developed by Disney in the 1990s, Celebration (ZIP 34747) was planned as a traditional small town. That's why the homes share such a strong look, and why exterior changes go through architectural review.

We've repainted Victorian homes in Celebration from top to bottom, including the blue and pink homes on this page. On homes like these we usually replace soft trim and siding before painting, so the porch looks right for years, not just for the photos.

Color approval in Celebration

Celebration's residential owners association (often called CROA) reviews exterior changes, including paint colors, through its architectural review process. Whether you call it your HOA or your POA, the rule is the same: get your colors approved before work starts.

  • Pick from the approved palette for your home's style
  • Submit your application and wait for approval
  • Send us the approval and we'll paint to it exactly

Repainting in the same approved colors? Check with the association. Some changes still need a form even when the color stays the same.

Celebration painting questions

Do I need HOA or POA approval to repaint my Celebration home?

Celebration's residential owners association reviews exterior changes, including paint colors. Check with them and get approval before work starts. We'll paint to your approved colors.

Can you repair the porch trim before painting?

Yes. Rotted porch trim, railings, gable details and siding are some of the most common repairs we make on Celebration homes, and we paint them to match.

How long does a Celebration exterior repaint take?

It depends on the size of the home, how much repair is needed and the weather. We'll give you a timeline with your written estimate.
