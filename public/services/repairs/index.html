Repair questions

How do I know if I have wood rot?

Look for soft or crumbly trim, bubbling paint, gaps at the bottom of the siding, or stains below windows. Call us and we'll take a look.

Will the repair match the rest of the house?

Yes. We match the siding profile and paint the repair so it blends in.
