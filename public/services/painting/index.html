Painting questions

Are estimates free?

Yes. We come out, look at the job and give you a written price before any work starts.

Do you repair wood rot before painting?

Yes. We replace soft trim, siding and sheathing first, then prime and paint so the repair disappears into the finish.

Do I need to be home while you paint?

Not for exterior work. We'll walk the job with you at the start and the end, and you can reach us by phone anytime.

Can you match my HOA's colors?

Yes. Bring us your approved color list and we'll match it.
