Vacation rental painting in Kissimmee and Reunion

Keep your short-term rental guest-ready. We paint, patch and wash between bookings, and we report back if you live out of town.

Guests notice everything

Scuffed hallways, chipped door frames, faded front doors and stained pool decks all show up in listing photos and guest reviews. Rental homes near the parks get far more wear than a typical home, so they need painting and small repairs more often.

We work on vacation homes in Kissimmee, Reunion and Encore at Reunion West. That means interior and exterior repaints, touch-ups, drywall and door repairs, and pressure washing for pool decks, lanais and driveways.

Vacation rental questions

Can you work while the home is between guests?

Yes. Send us your open dates. Touch-ups and single rooms can often fit into a short gap, and bigger repaints are planned around a longer block.

I live out of state. How does the estimate work?

Send photos and a list, or have your property manager let us in for a walkthrough. We'll send a written estimate, and photos when the work is done.

Do you work in Encore at Reunion West and other resort communities?

Yes. We painted the exterior of 450 Burma in Encore at Reunion West. We also work in Reunion, ChampionsGate, Davenport and Kissimmee.

Will you follow my HOA or POA's rules?

Yes. Get exterior color changes approved by your association first, and we'll paint to the approved colors.
