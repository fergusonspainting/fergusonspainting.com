Carpentry questions

What wood do you use for decks?

Pressure-treated lumber for the framing, with deck boards chosen for your budget and the look you want. We'll go over the options at the estimate.

Can you paint or stain the deck too?

Yes. We build it and finish it, so you deal with one crew from start to finish.

Do you repair existing decks and porches?

Yes. We replace rotted boards, posts and railings and match the existing look.
