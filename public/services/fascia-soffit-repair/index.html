Fascia and soffit repair in Orlando

Rotted fascia boards, sagging soffits and damaged gable trim, replaced and painted to match.

Why fascia and soffits rot

Fascia boards sit right at the roof's edge, behind the gutters, where Florida's afternoon storms hit hardest. Clogged or overflowing gutters, gaps in paint and caulk, and constant humidity let water soak into the wood. Once it softens, rot spreads along the board and into the soffit. It can also open a path for insects and animals.

Signs to look for

  • Peeling or bubbling paint along the roofline
  • Soft spots when you press the wood
  • Sagging or gapping soffit panels
  • Dark stains or mildew under the eaves
  • Wasps or critters getting in near the roof

How we repair it

  • Remove every rotted section, not just the visibly bad spot
  • Check the rafter tails and sheathing behind it
  • Install new fascia, soffit or trim to match the profile
  • Prime all sides and caulk the joints
  • Paint to match the house

Fascia and soffit questions

Can you just paint over the rotted fascia?

No. Paint can't hold soft wood together, and the rot keeps spreading underneath. The rotted section has to be replaced.

Will the new boards match?

Yes. We match the profile of the existing trim and paint it to match the house.
