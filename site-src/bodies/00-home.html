Common questions

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We look at the job, in person or from your photos, and give you a written price with no obligation.

Are you insured?

Yes. Ferguson's Painting & Cleaning, Inc. is fully insured, and we'll send the certificate on request.

Do you repair rot and cracks before painting?

Yes. Wood rot, stucco cracks and drywall damage are fixed by the same crew before the paint goes on. See wood rot repair and stucco repair.

Where do you work?

Orlando and the communities around it: Kissimmee, Celebration, Reunion, ChampionsGate, Davenport, Windermere, Dr. Phillips, Winter Park, Lake Mary and more. See all service areas.

Will you follow my HOA or POA's color rules?

Yes. Get your colors approved by your association first, and we'll paint to the approved specs. We also help with violation notices for mildew or faded paint.

How fast will you reply?

Usually within one business day, Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm. Call or text (321) 202-7225 if it's urgent.
