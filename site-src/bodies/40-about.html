Meet Derwin

Derwin Ferguson started Ferguson's Painting & Cleaning to give Central Florida homeowners a contractor they could count on to show up, tell them the truth about their house, and leave it cleaner than they found it.

What began as painting and pressure washing grew into full home maintenance: carpentry, pool decks, wood-rot and siding repair, and bathroom remodels. Customers kept asking, "Can you do this too?" More often than not, the answer was yes.

Derwin is on the job himself. When you call, you talk to the person doing the work.
