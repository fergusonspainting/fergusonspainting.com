Color approval in Celebration

Celebration's residential owners association (often called CROA) reviews exterior changes, including paint colors, through its architectural review process. Whether you call it your HOA or your POA, the rule is the same: get your colors approved before work starts.

  • Pick from the approved palette for your home's style
  • Submit your application and wait for approval
  • Send us the approval and we'll paint to it exactly

Repainting in the same approved colors? Check with the association. Some changes still need a form even when the color stays the same.
