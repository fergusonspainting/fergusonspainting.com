Drywall repair questions

Will I be able to see the patch?

Not if it's done right. We match the texture and prime before painting. Sometimes the best result means repainting the whole wall, so the sheen and color match edge to edge.

Can you fix water-damaged drywall?

Yes, once the leak has been repaired and the area has dried. We cut out the damaged section, replace it, then tape, texture, prime and paint.

Do you take small drywall jobs?

Yes. Group a few repairs together, or pair them with painting, to get the most out of one visit. See handyman services.
