Painting Winter Park homes

Winter Park, just north of Orlando in ZIP codes 32789 and 32792, is known for Park Avenue, its chain of lakes and tree-lined brick streets. Many homes are older, with wood siding, detailed trim, porches and original windows that need real prep, not just a fresh coat.

Old wood under deep shade and lake humidity is a recipe for rot and peeling. We replace soft wood, scrape and prime properly, and paint so the details stay crisp. Everything around the work is protected, and the job site is cleaned every day.

Winter Park questions

Do you work on older and historic homes?

Yes. Older wood homes need careful prep and often some rot repair. We handle both before painting.

My home may have old lead paint. What should I know?

Homes built before 1978 may have lead-based paint. Have it tested before any sanding or scraping. If lead is present, federal rules require the work to be done by an EPA lead-safe certified renovator.
