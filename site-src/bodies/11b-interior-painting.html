Neat and clean, inside your home

Working inside someone's home takes extra care. Before we open a can:

  • Furniture is moved or covered
  • Floors are covered with drop cloths
  • Outlets, switches and fixtures are masked
  • Holes and cracks are patched and sanded

At the end of each day we clean up, so you can still live in the house while we work.

Interior painting questions

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. We move and cover furniture as part of the job. If you have fragile or valuable pieces, let us know and we'll plan around them.

Can you fix holes and cracks before painting?

Yes. We patch nail holes, dents and drywall damage, sand them smooth and prime them, so they don't show through the new paint.

Can I stay in the house while you paint?

Yes. We work room by room and clean up at the end of each day.

What paint do you use?

Derwin's go-to is Sherwin-Williams. If you have a favorite brand or a specific color line, just tell us and we'll use it.

Do you paint stores and offices?

Yes. We can work after hours so your business stays open. See commercial painting.
