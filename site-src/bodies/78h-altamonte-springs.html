Painting Altamonte Springs homes

Altamonte Springs, in Seminole County between Maitland and Longwood (ZIP codes 32701 and 32714), is home to Cranes Roost Park and Uptown Altamonte. Many of its neighborhoods were built from the 1970s through the 1990s, with stucco and block homes, townhomes and plenty of HOA or POA communities.

Homes of that age usually need more than paint: stucco cracks, tired trim, dated popcorn ceilings and worn interiors. We take care of the repairs and the paint together.
