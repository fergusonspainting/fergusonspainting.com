Remodeling questions

Can you handle the painting after the remodel?

Yes. That's our trade. Walls, ceilings, trim and doors get painted as part of the job, so there's no second contractor to schedule.

Is vinyl plank good for Florida homes?

Waterproof vinyl plank stands up well to humidity, pets and sandy feet, which makes it a popular choice here.

Do you do kitchen backsplashes?

We remove old backsplashes and repair the drywall behind them. Ask us about the new backsplash at your estimate.
