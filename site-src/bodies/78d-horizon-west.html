Painting Horizon West homes

Horizon West is a fast-growing, master-planned area in west Orange County, between Winter Garden, Windermere and the Disney area, mostly in ZIP 34787. It's organized into villages, such as Hamlin and Lakeshore, made up of newer stucco homes in HOA or POA communities.
