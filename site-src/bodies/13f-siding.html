Siding repair questions

Can you match my existing siding?

Yes. We match the board profile and exposure, then prime and paint so the repair blends in.

What causes siding to rot?

Usually water: sprinklers hitting the wall, gutters overflowing, failed caulk around windows, or siding sitting too close to the ground or a roof.

Do I need to repaint the whole house?

Not always. We can paint the repaired section to match. If the rest of the paint is worn, a full repaint gives the most even result. See exterior painting.
